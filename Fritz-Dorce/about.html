Fritz Dorce

Founder, ITD Insurance That Delivers

ITD Insurance That Delivers is an independent insurance practice founded in South Florida by Fritz Dorce, a licensed agent devoted to protecting what matters most to local families and businesses. Fritz built ITD on a simple principle: insurance shouldn't feel like a maze. Every quote, every policy and every claim is handled personally — the same licensed agent, the same phone number, year after year.

As an independent agent, Fritz is appointed with carriers including Aflac, Americo, ManhattanLife and United of Omaha. Licensed in Florida, Georgia and Texas across life, personal and commercial lines, he evaluates each client's needs and compares real options — so you get a real recommendation instead of one company's playbook.
